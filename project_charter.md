# ElectPath Project Charter

## 1. Mission Statement
ElectPath is dedicated to providing a professional, non-partisan, and universally accessible election education platform. Our mission is to empower citizens with objective, verified information regarding the electoral process, completely free from political bias or external influence. We believe that an informed electorate is the foundation of a healthy democracy.

## 2. Project Scope
The ElectPath Minimum Viable Product (MVP) will focus on delivering an interactive, step-by-step un-biased guide to the election cycle.

**In-Scope:**
- **Interactive Election Timeline:** A progressively disclosed educational journey covering Registration → Campaign → Voting → Results.
- **Civic Knowledge Verification:** Short, non-partisan quizzes at the end of each module to validate user understanding.
- **Fact-Verification Pipeline:** Protocol for cross-referencing information with government databases to ensure information integrity.
- **Accessible UI:** A WCAG-compliant frontend built with React, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion.

**Out-of-Scope (for MVP):**
- Real-time polling data aggregation.
- Candidate endorsements or sentiment analysis.
- Third-party social media integrations that could introduce bias.

## 3. Target User Personas
1. **First-Time Voter (The Learner):** A young adult recently eligible to vote. They are enthusiastic but lack a fundamental understanding of the voter registration process, polling locations, and how to evaluate ballot measures neutrally. Needs clear, step-by-step guidance.
2. **The Passive Citizen (The Refresher):** Someone who votes sporadically. They are busy and easily overwhelmed by political noise. Needs a quick, reliable summary of dates, deadlines, and core election mechanics without partisan framing.
3. **The Accessibility-Focused User:** A user who relies on screen readers or requires specific contrast/text-sizing to navigate the web. Needs a deeply accessible interface that doesn't compromise on functionality or aesthetic quality.
